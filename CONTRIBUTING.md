# Contributing

When adding or changing project documentation:

1. Say which hardware revision the notes apply to.
2. Label measurements and wiring as **tested**, **estimated**, or **not yet verified**.
3. Include photos or diagrams for assembly changes.
4. Update the parts list when hardware changes.
5. Update the firmware notes when pin assignments or controls change.
6. Do not remove safety warnings that affect power, motion, welding, or fabrication.
