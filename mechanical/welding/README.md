# Welding and Metalwork

Document any welded frame, brackets, rods, or other metal structure here. If the build uses no welded parts, mark this section **not applicable** rather than leaving readers to guess.

## Files to add

- Dimensioned frame drawing (`.pdf`, `.svg`, or image)
- Editable CAD file if available
- Cut list and material specifications
- Photos of tack-up, fit checks, and finished welds

## Assembly notes

1. Confirm all dimensions against the actual printed parts and case/head assembly.
2. Make a dry fit and check alignment before final welding.
3. Record weld locations and any grinding/finishing.
4. Check that sharp edges cannot damage fabric, wires, or the wearer.
5. Add photos and the final measured dimensions.

Use appropriate welding PPE, ventilation, and fire precautions. Do not weld near batteries, electronics, fabric, or other flammable materials.
